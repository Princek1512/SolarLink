# 5-Minute Hackathon Demo

## Scenario

Use two prosumers and one consumer.

### 0:00–0:40 — Setup
Show login and configured zones/pricing band.

### 0:40–1:20 — IoT
Start simulated solar generation. Show generation and consumption. Surplus appears automatically.

### 1:20–2:00 — Marketplace
Open consumer marketplace. Show available kWh and dynamic price.

### 2:00–2:40 — Buy + Match
Consumer buys energy. Matching engine selects nearby supply.

### 2:40–3:20 — Ledger
Show trade lifecycle:

`LOCKED → DELIVERED → VERIFIED`

Display tamper-evident event history.

### 3:20–4:00 — Settlement
Show buyer debit and prosumer credit.

### 4:00–4:40 — Congestion
Increase zone load above threshold. New trade becomes surcharged/paused.

### 4:40–5:00 — Dashboard
Show energy traded, earnings, consumer savings, congestion status, and compliance/audit event.

## Backup Scenario

If blockchain or realtime services fail, demonstrate the hash-chain ledger and simulator using seeded data.
